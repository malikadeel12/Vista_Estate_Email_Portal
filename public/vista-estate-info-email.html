ISA Verification Process

Every lead is personally contacted and verified by our trained ISA team before being referred to an agent. We confirm the prospect's contact information, buying or selling intent, timeline, property details, and level of motivation. We also obtain their consent to be connected with a real estate professional, ensuring that every lead delivered has been qualified, verified, and approved for transfer.

Our ISA team asks key qualifying questions:

  • Are they pre-approved?
  • What is their credit score?
  • Do they have any bankruptcies or foreclosures?
  • What is their annual income and employment status?
  • Are they currently under contract with a realtor?

This ensures we deliver motivated, intent-verified buyers and sellers.
